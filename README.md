# 🚀 About FIN-INTEL OS

**FIN-INTEL OS** is a multi-agent financial intelligence platform primarily designed for **retail investors**.

Retail investors often need to analyze several factors before making an investment decision, including market movements, company fundamentals, financial news, sentiment, and risk.

FIN-INTEL OS brings these different perspectives together into a single platform.

The system uses multiple specialized financial agents to analyze different aspects of a stock and a **Synthesis Arbiter** to combine their outputs into an explainable financial insight.

The platform also provides a stock dashboard, watchlist, peer comparison, paper trading, agent audit trail, and tactical reminders.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Retail investors have access to a large amount of financial information, but making sense of that information can be difficult.

An investor may need to:

- Analyze market movements
- Study company fundamentals
- Read financial news
- Understand market sentiment
- Evaluate risk
- Compare companies
- Consider portfolio exposure

These activities are often performed across multiple platforms.

### The problem

> **How can we help retail investors bring multiple financial perspectives together and understand the reasoning behind an investment insight?**

FIN-INTEL OS addresses this problem using a **multi-agent financial intelligence architecture**.
